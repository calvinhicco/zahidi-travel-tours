Your Journey Our Priority

Zahidi Travel & Tours plans holidays, school excursions, corporate retreats and international city breaks from Zimbabwe. The navy-and-orange mark — a globe-orbit Z with an aircraft — is the same identity on our shirts, vans and flyers.

We know Victoria Falls, Lake Kariba, Nyanga, Chinhoyi Caves and Mutarazi. We also sell Dubai, Abu Dhabi, London and Singapore with flights, hotels, visas and insurance under one enquiry.
