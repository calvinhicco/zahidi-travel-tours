Destinations

Zimbabwe first. Then the world.

Matabeleland North

Victoria Falls

Stand in the spray of Mosi-oa-Tunya, walk the rainforest trails, and look down the Zambezi gorge from the bridge. Zahidi arranges falls tours, helicopter flips, sunset cruises, bungee, white-water rafting and lodge stays on both the Zimbabwe and Zambia sides.

Best with: 2–4 nights · couples, families, school groups

Mashonaland West

Lake Kariba

Africa’s great inland sea. Houseboats drift past drowned forests, tiger fish pull at the line, and elephants come down to drink at dusk. We book lodges, houseboats, fishing charters and game-viewing around Kariba town, Matusadona and the eastern basin.

Best with: weekend getaways · corporate retreats · fishing parties

Eastern Highlands

Nyanga holiday

Cool mountain air, lodge pools, trout streams and hiking trails. Stay in the highlands with views over Nyanga National Park, then add Mutarazi skywalk, waterfall walks and quiet evenings on the lawn. A favourite for families, couples and long weekends from Harare.

Day trips from Harare

Chinhoyi Caves & lion park

A Zahidi favourite for schools and families. Descend to the Sleeping Pool, take the caves walk, then continue to live lion viewing. We provide branded transport, packed itineraries and group coordination — as seen on our recent Tree of Life and school tours.
